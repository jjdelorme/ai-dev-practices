RECURSIVE HARNESSING

Harness²: Recursive Agent Harnessing

Optimizing the System Around a Frozen Model for an Open World
  • Evolving the Harness, Not the Weights: 8 editable components around a fixed model. Exposes system prompt, guardrails, memory, skills, tool guidance, subagents, scripts, and plugins as editable artifacts controlling context, tools, delegation, and middleware.
  • Task-Level Recursion (Propose–Probe–Compose): Zero-label contrastive refinement. For unseen open-world tasks, an editor proposes invariant and domain edits, candidate probes execute them, and a composer synthesizes a refined harness from behavioral contrasts—without ground-truth feedback.
  • Harnessing-Level Recursion: Persistent cross-task meta-learning. An experience reflector distills adopted harnesses and evidence-backed lessons into a persistent improvement harness that accelerates future task adaptations.
  • Parallel & Sequential Scaling (--k): Explore wider or refine deeper. Scale test-time harness search in parallel or sequential steps across OpenCode and Codex executors—boosting Gemini 3.5 Flash & GLM-5 by up to +11.5 pts on WorkBuddy-Bench and +11.8% on LAB.
