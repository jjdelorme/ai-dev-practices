THE COMMAND SPECTRUM

Choosing the Right Agentic Workflow

Tier 1: Single-Agent Control Loops • Tier 2: Multi-Agent Orchestration
/grill-me Single Agent
1. Interactive Design Interview

Interviews you one question at a time (with recommended defaults) to resolve requirements and trade-offs before work starts.

When to use: Requirements are fuzzy and you want to flesh out edge cases interactively.

/plan Single Agent
2. Spec-First Approval Gate

Researches the codebase and drafts a structured implementation & verification plan artifact (Proceed to approve) before touching code.

When to use: You want human sign-off on the technical design before execution.

/goal Single Agent Loop
3. Persistent Verification Loop

Native “Ralph Loop” that intercepts premature turn completion and forces iterative continuation until explicit verification gates pass.

When to use: Grinding through test suites, coverage targets, or refactors until 100% green.

/boost 2-Layer Multi-Agent
4. Adversarial Worker + Critic Pipeline

Dispatches a worker subagent followed immediately by an adversarial reviewer subagent that stress-tests, verifies, and fixes the result with zero upfront interview.

When to use: Tackling a gnarly bug, root-cause investigation, or tricky single-PR feature that needs extra rigor on the first try.

/teamwork Multi-Hour Multi-Agent
5. Autonomous Project Squad

Runs a structured scoping interview to lock acceptance criteria, then launches an autonomous multi-agent team with progress tracking and an independent auditor.

When to use: Delegating large, multi-component migrations, greenfield builds, or hours-long projects end-to-end.
