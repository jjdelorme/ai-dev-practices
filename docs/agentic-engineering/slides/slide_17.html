THE /TEAMWORK WORKFLOW

How /teamwork Executes

Three Structured Phases from Alignment to Verified Delivery
STEP 1 01
Scope & Approve

Runs a structured scoping interview to lock requirements, acceptance criteria, and milestone boundaries upfront.

Gate: Human signs off on the scope before the autonomous squad launches.

STEP 2 02
Build & Challenge

Coordinator dispatches explorers and builders across milestones while adversarial critics challenge every change.

Loop: Critics stress-test edge cases and reject weak implementations automatically.

STEP 3 03
Audit & Deliver

An independent auditor verifies the complete build, test suite, and traceability against the original spec.

Output: Verified code + clean completion report ready for review.
