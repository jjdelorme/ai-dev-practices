The Critical Thinking Gap

Preserving Cognition in an Automated Workflow
  • Cognitive Atrophy: Delegation erodes your ability to reason. If you delegate everything to the AI without active engagement, your architectural muscles will atrophy.
  • Sparring Partner: Use AI to challenge your own logic. Don't just take answers; ask the model to outline alternatives and criticize your decisions.
  • Verification First: Only delegate what you can verify. If you cannot inspect, run, and guarantee the safety of the output, do not push it to production.
  • Keep Your Edge: Ask for "Why," not just "What." Always ask models to explain their rationale, trade-offs, and structural decisions.
