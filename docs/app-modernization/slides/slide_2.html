PHASE I • CAPTURE & SPECIFY

Steps 0 & 1: Baseline Tests & OKF Specification

Lock Down Legacy Behavior First, Then Extract Tech-Agnostic Business Capabilities
STEP 0 • PRE-REQUISITES 00
Behavioral Baseline in a Lower Env
  • Lower-Environment Access: Stand up the running legacy app where agents can safely inspect workflows. Gives the team an executable ground-truth oracle before touching code.
  • Characterization Test Suite: Capture real inputs, outputs, and edge cases across core scenarios. Locks down existing behavior to drive Step 3 parity verification.
  • Product & App Owner Alignment: Clarify intent with stakeholders in the room. Decide early where to match vs. reimagine UI and merge duplicate flows.

Exit Gate: Running lower-env instance + executable characterization scenarios.

STEP 1 • SPECIFICATION 01
Business Capabilities in OKF
  • Tech-Agnostic OKF: Document pure business capabilities in Open Knowledge Format. Deliberately elide legacy frameworks, libraries, and implementation details.
  • Domain-Driven Design (DDD): Model the domain cleanly from first principles. Define bounded contexts, core entities, invariants, and capabilities.
  • Gherkin & Target Stack Rules: Express behavior as verifiable scenarios paired with stack constraints. Bundle foundational org rules (target framework, approved DB) for Step 2 ingestion.

Exit Gate: Approved, framework-free OKF spec read piece-by-piece on demand.
